01 · SYSTEM OVERVIEW

What you're plugging into

Figures reflect Techfest's most recent published program data.

Techfest, IIT Bombay is Asia's largest science and technology festival — held under the patronage of bodies including UNESCO and UNICEF, and backed by national initiatives like Make in India and Digital India. It draws students, innovators, and researchers from across the globe onto one campus for three days.

The College Ambassador Program is the pan-India network that makes that reach possible — a body of tech-savvy student representatives who carry Techfest's brand into their own institutions, act as the first point of contact for their peers, and get scored on a live leaderboard for real internship credit in return.
